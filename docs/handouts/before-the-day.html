Before the Day

Four things need to be true before you walk in. Each one is written as something you can prove, because the failure this sheet exists to prevent is arriving believing you are set up when you are not.

Why you are being asked for forty minutes

The day is spent building something you actually need, and there is no time in it for installation. More importantly: the ways this goes wrong at the start are silent. An agent that cannot open your files will still answer questions about them, fluently and confidently, from whatever you pasted — and you will not be able to tell from the reply.

So every item below has a check attached. Doing the step is not the same as the step having worked, and that distinction is the subject of the whole day.

The four

  1. You have an agent, and you are signed in with an account you mean to keep.

    How you know
    Ask it for a file, and a file comes back. “Make a file called hello.txt with the word hello in it, and show me the file.”
    If instead you get
    Instructions for making one yourself, a blank template to fill in, or a new app to click through — you have the wrong kind of product. That is not a mistake you made, and it is not fixable on the morning. Say so now.
    On the account
    A throwaway you sign up with in a hurry becomes the one every piece of your work hangs off. Moving later means moving all of it.

  2. The agent can see files on your own computer.

    How you know
    Point it at your Documents folder — or any folder you actually keep files in — and ask which three files changed most recently. “Which three files in this folder changed most recently?” Then sort that folder by date in your file browser and look at the top three. They have to be the same three.
    You will know at a glance if it is wrong
    A chat window will answer with three confident, plausible, entirely invented filenames — and you will recognise instantly that they are not yours. That is why the question asks for names and not a number: an invented count is unfalsifiable in practice, an invented filename is obviously wrong the moment you read it. This is the whole day in one minute — the answer looked right, and looking right is not the test.
    It does not read your files
    This needs the file names and their dates only, never what is inside them. If the folder you pick holds data that should not be read by anything, this check still does not read it.

  3. You have written down a real problem, in one paragraph.

    How you know
    A colleague could read it and tell you what the finished thing would do.
    What makes a good one
    Something you have already failed to get out of a chat window is the best possible choice. A figure that came back described instead of drawn; a table you wanted reshaped; a calculation you gave up explaining. You will leave with the thing itself, so bring a problem you would actually use the answer to.

  4. You have whatever that problem needs, on your laptop.

    How you know
    The file opens, on your machine, with the network turned off.
    Why offline
    A file that lives on shared storage or a sync service opens beautifully at your desk and not always in a seminar room. Copy what you need onto the laptop itself.

If any of the four does not come true

Tell us before the day rather than on it. Every one of these is fixable with a few minutes of warning and none of them is fixable at nine in the morning with twenty people waiting. Nobody has ever been the only person on the list.

Bring

  • The laptop you did the four checks on — not a different one.
  • Its charger.
  • The paragraph, and the data.

Nothing else. There is no reading to do, and no account to set up on the morning.

If four conditions are not enough

Everything above is the short version, and the long one already exists. Cold Start is the full setup runbook — every account, every install, and the same style of check on each one. Open it if something here will not come true and you would rather work it out than wait for the morning.

You are not expected to read it, and most of it is not about this day. It goes on past the four conditions into publishing a page on the internet and putting your own domain name in front of it, which the workshop does not touch. Take the parts you need and leave the rest.
